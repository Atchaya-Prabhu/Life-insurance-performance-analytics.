## 📌 Project Overview

An exploratory financial analytics project focused on evaluating the financial performance of Canadian life insurance companies using aggregate financial reporting data.

Using Python, this project examines trends in insurance revenue, insurance service expenses, and reported insurance service results across fiscal quarters. It combines **data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), KPI development, data visualization, correlation analysis, and exploratory forecasting** to identify financial patterns and support data-driven insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- 📈 Analyze historical trends in insurance revenue, insurance service expenses, and reported insurance service results.
- ⚖️ Evaluate revenue and expense growth across comparable fiscal periods.
- 💹 Assess changes in the reported insurance service result relative to total insurance revenue.
- 🔗 Explore relationships between selected financial measures.
- 🔮 Investigate historical trends using a simple forecasting approach.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 💰 **Total Insurance Revenue** | Measures reported insurance revenue for a given fiscal period. |
| 📉 **Insurance Service Expenses** | Tracks reported expenses associated with insurance services. |
| 🧾 **Reported Insurance Service Result** | Evaluates the reported insurance service result for the period. |
| 📈 **Revenue Growth (%)** | Measures the percentage change in revenue between comparable periods. |
| ⚖️ **Expense Growth (%)** | Measures the percentage change in insurance service expenses. |
| 💹 **Service Result Growth (%)** | Measures the percentage change in the reported insurance service result. |
| 🎯 **Service Result-to-Revenue Ratio (%)** | Measures the reported insurance service result as a percentage of total insurance revenue. |

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

<details>
<summary><strong>📈 Revenue Growth — 22.3%</strong></summary>

Total insurance revenue increased from **71.76 million in Q4 2023** to **87.75 million in Q4 2025**, representing approximately 22.3% growth.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>📉 Insurance Service Expenses — 21.9% Growth</strong></summary>

Insurance service expenses increased from **58.46 million in Q4 2023** to **71.26 million in Q4 2025**, representing approximately 21.9% growth.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>💹 Reported Insurance Service Result — 24.1% Growth</strong></summary>

The reported insurance service result increased from **9.99 million in Q4 2023** to **12.39 million in Q4 2025**, representing approximately 24.1% growth.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>🎯 Service Result-to-Revenue Ratio</strong></summary>

The ratio declined from **13.92% in Q4 2023** to **13.50% in Q4 2024**, before recovering to **14.13% in Q4 2025**.

</details>

### 💡 Key Takeaway

Revenue and the reported insurance service result increased over the two-year period. The service result-to-revenue ratio also recovered in 2025 after declining in 2024.

These observations describe historical trends and do not establish the underlying causes of the changes.

> **Comparison period:** Fourth-quarter (Q4) results for 2023, 2024, and 2025.
